What a policy includes

  • Vet treatment for accidents and illness, up to ${{ '{:,.0f}'.format(coverage) }} a year
  • Claims submitted from the app, with a photo of the vet bill
  • A clear decision on every claim, visible in the app

How a claim works

  1. Add your pet and start cover at the price shown.
  2. After a vet visit, submit a claim in the app with what happened, the amount and a photo of the bill.
  3. We review it, and the claim's status changes to approved or rejected.

This is a demo project: no real insurance is sold and no claims are paid out.
