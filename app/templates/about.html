About Nami

Nami makes pet insurance easy to understand: an instant monthly price, one clear limit, and claims you can follow from your phone.

It is a portfolio project by Batistuta Indiazi, built with a Flask website and API and a Flutter app for Android. No real insurance is sold and no claims are paid out.

Questions or feedback are welcome on the contact page.
