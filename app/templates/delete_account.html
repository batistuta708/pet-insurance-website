Delete your Nami Pet Insurance account

You can permanently delete your account and all of its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open the app and log in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email batistutatinami@gmail.com from the email address you registered with and ask for your account to be deleted. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and password)
  • Your pets and their policies
  • Your claims, including any photos you attached

Nothing is kept after deletion.
