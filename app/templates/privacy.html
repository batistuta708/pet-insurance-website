Privacy policy

{# Review this text before publishing on Google Play. #}

This policy covers the Nami Pet Insurance website and mobile app. This is a demo project: no real insurance is sold or provided.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Pet details: the name, type and age of pets you add.
  • Claims: the description, amount and any photo (for example a vet bill) you attach to a claim.

How we use it

Only to provide the service: calculating quotes, managing your policies and processing claims. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Storage and security

Data is stored on our server and sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. The mobile app keeps your sign-in token in the device's secure storage.

Deleting your data

You can delete pets, or your whole account, from the app at any time. See how to delete your account, including without the app.
